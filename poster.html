The World
as a Canvas

Materializing Imagination through Prompting the Physical World

UIST '26 Full-Day Workshop
November 2, 2026 · 09:00 – 17:00 · Detroit Marriott at the Renaissance Center, Detroit, MI

Generative AI is rapidly lowering the barriers to creation, yet XR introduces fundamentally different challenges as creation moves beyond flat screens into the physical world. XR experiences must account for real spaces, embodied and multimodal interaction, collaboration across heterogeneous devices, and content that unfolds through space and time. This workshop brings together researchers and practitioners to examine these challenges and develop a shared vision for more accessible, expressive, and collaborative XR creation.
